## What are birds ?

- Feathered Bipeds.
- Endothermic vertebrates, with feathers, toothless beaks, a four-chambered heart ..
- The only living dinosaurs
- Reptilia
	- Crocodiles
	- Dinosauria
		- Theropoda
			- Coelosauria
				- Tyrranasauroidea
				- Maniraptora
				- Aves (birds)
	- Turtles
	- Snakes
- Birds are everywhere.
- Birds are diverse.
- Flight is a particular interest.

## Why study birds ?

- Birds advance science:
	- Evolution and speciation
	- Populations, Communities, Co-existence 
	- Foraging and reproductive decisions
	- Social behavior
	- Song and migration
- Birds inspire engineering
- Birds serve ecosystems and us
- Birds are indicators

## Ornithology in India

- Various Sanskrit words referring to species of birds in scriptures.
- Mughal emperor Jahangir was interested in birds and commissioned paintings one of which captures the Dodo and Siberian crane.
- The modern ornithology started during colonial period in India. 
	- Birds of India book was published.
	- Based on specimen collection.
	- Done primarily by the British officials as hobby.
- Salim Ali was one of the first Indian ornithologist
	- Famous books: *The book of Indian birds* and *Handbook of the birds of India and Pakistan*.
	- Based on observation of live birds.
