# Wing Design 
*Created on:* 2024-12-01

## Definition
*Wing design is the first step of detail design. The wing must produce sufficient lift while generating minimum drag and minimum pitching moment.*

Wing reference area (S) is obtained in [[Preliminary Design]]. Other parameters are obtained here,
- *Number of wings*: 1, 2 or 3
- *Wing location*: High, Mid or Low
	- General aircraft and cargo have high wing
	- Passenger aircrafts have low wings
	- Most fighter aircrafts and some GA have mid wing.
- *Airfoil Design*
	- Higher $C_{L_{0}}$
	- Lower variation of $C_{L_{i}}$
	- Docile stall qualities
	- Lower $C_{D_{0}}$
	- Higher L/D
	- Lowest $C_{m_{0}}$
	- Ideal lift coefficient obtained from cruise
	- Maximum lift coefficient obtained from stall and use of HLD
- *Wing Incidence*
	- AoA at which ideal lift coefficient is generated
	- Drag coefficient is minimum during cruising flight
- *Aspect Ratio*
	- Increase in AR , 3 D wing ---> 2 D airfoil.
	- Weight increases with AR.
	- L/D increases with AR.
	- Selected based on past aircraft data.
- *Taper Ratio*
	- Introduced to obtain elliptical lift distribution and reduce the induced drag.
	- Tip may stall before the root (Undesirable).
	- Increase the cost of wing manufacturing
	- Reduces the weight of the wing.
- *Sweep Angle*
	- Angle between a constant chord line and the lateral axis perpendicular to the fuselage centerline.
	- Improve aerodynamic performance at higher flight speeds.
	- Adjusting CG.
	- Improving static stability.
	- Tip stall may produce severe stability issues.
	- Low subsonic aircraft, no sweep recommended.
	- High subsonic and supersonic aircraft, must have sweep.
- *Twist Angle*
	- Aerodynamic and geometric twist.
	- Aerodynamic twist more practical and convenient than geometric twist.
	- Twist results in reduction of lift.
	- Done to obtain an elliptic lift distribution.
- *Dihedral Angle*
	- Improves lateral stability of the aircraft.
	- Prevents excessive spiral instability, but Dutch-roll becomes instable.
	- For high-wing aircrafts, anhedral is used to reduce the excessive stability.
	- The balance between lateral stability and roll control is a major criterion for the determination of dihedral angle.
	- More stability ==> Less control
- *High Lift Devices*
	- To maximize the $C_{L_{max}}$.
	- Leading edge and trailing edge HLD
	- HLD chord, span, maximum deflection, type and location along span depends on the mission and control surfaces.
