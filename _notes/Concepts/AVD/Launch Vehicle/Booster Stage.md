## Definition
*Booster stages are used in launch vehicles to increase the thrust of the launch vehicles.*

Total mass of the launch vehicle
$$
m_{0} = m_{p,booster} + m_{s,booster} + m_{01}
$$

Mass flow rate of the launch vehicle
$$
\dot{m}_{e,0} = \dot{m}_{e,booster} + \dot{m}_{e,01}
$$
Exhaust velocity of the launch vehicle

$$
V_{e,0} = \frac{\dot{m}_{e,booster}V_{e,booster} + \dot{m}_{e,01}V_{e,01}}{\dot{m}_{e,booster} + \dot{m}_{e,01}}
$$
