# Primary Flight Instruments 
*Created on:* 2025-01-20  
*Tags:* #FlightInstruments
*Course:* [[Flight Mechanics]]

---
 These instruments provide essential information for piloting an aircraft.
 
- **Altimeter**
    - Measures altitude above a reference level (typically sea level) using static air pressure.
    - Operates on the principle of decreasing atmospheric pressure with increasing altitude.
    - Displays altitude on a dial in feet or meters.
- **Airspeed Indicator (ASI)**
    - Measures the aircraft's speed relative to the surrounding air.
    - Operates using the difference between static pressure and dynamic pressure from the Pitot tube system.
    - Indicates speed in knots or mph, with critical speeds like V1 (takeoff decision speed) and VNE (never exceed speed) marked.
- **Vertical Speed Indicator (VSI)**
    - Shows the rate of climb or descent in feet per minute (fpm) or meters per second.
    - Uses the rate of change of static pressure to determine vertical velocity.
    - Has a lag time, so it is not instantaneous.
- **Turn-Bank Indicator**
    - Displays the rate of turn and the quality of the turn (coordinated or uncoordinated).
    - Consists of two components:
        - **Turn Coordinator**: Shows the rate and direction of the turn.
        - **Inclinometer (Slip-Skid Indicator)**: Ensures coordinated flight using a ball in a curved tube.
