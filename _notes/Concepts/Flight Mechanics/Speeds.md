# Speeds 
*Created on:* 2025-01-20

## True Airspeed

- Based on the measurement of static and total pressure through a pitot-static tube, we can determine the true airspeed of the aircraft.
$$
V_{true} = \sqrt{ \frac{2(p_{0}-p)}{\rho} }
$$

## Indicated or Equivalent Airspeed

- Read from air speed indicator (ASI).
- Due to difficulty in measurement of density at the airplane's location, we calibrate it based on the density at standard sea-level $\rho_{s}$.
- This is known as equivalent airspeed.
$$
V_{e} = \sqrt{ \frac{2(p_{0}-p)}{\rho_{s}} }
$$

## Calibrated Airspeed

- The airspeed is calibrated to accommodate for the errors in measurement of pressure difference.
- It also accounts for the position of the pitot-static tube fixed onto the aircraft.
