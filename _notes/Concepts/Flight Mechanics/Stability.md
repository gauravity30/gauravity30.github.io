## Definition

- *[[Static Stability]]*: If the forces and moments on the body caused by a disturbance tend initially to return toward its equilibrium position, the body is statically stable.
- *Trim*: The moments of the aircraft about the center of gravity is zero.
- *Dynamic Stability*: A body is dynamically stable if, of its own accord, it eventually returns to and remains at its equilibrium position over time.
	- *Aperiodic Oscillations*: Monotonically returns to the equilibrium.
	- *Damped Oscillations*: Oscillates around the equilibrium position and eventually returns to it.
- *Control*: Allows the airplane to change from one equilibrium position to another position.
