## Definition

Aircraft orientation is controlled using three axes:

- **Roll (Longitudinal Axis)**
    - Controlled by **ailerons** on the wings.
    - Causes the aircraft to tilt to the left or right, initiating a turn.
- **Pitch (Lateral Axis)**
    - Controlled by the **elevator** on the horizontal stabilizer.
    - Adjusts the nose-up or nose-down attitude, influencing climb and descent.
- **Yaw (Vertical Axis)**
    - Controlled by the **rudder** on the vertical stabilizer.
    - Adjusts the nose's direction left or right, aligning with the flight path.
