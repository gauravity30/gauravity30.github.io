## Definition

Four fundamental forces act on an aircraft during flight:

- **Lift**
    - Acts perpendicular to the relative wind and supports the aircraft against gravity.
    - Generated primarily by the wings, influenced by AoA, airspeed, and wing design.
- **Drag**
    - Acts opposite to the relative wind, resisting motion.
    - Types:
        - **Parasite Drag**: Includes form drag, skin friction, and interference drag.
        - **Induced Drag**: Associated with lift generation.
- **Thrust**
    - Generated by the engine and propeller or jet system.
    - Overcomes drag and propels the aircraft forward.
- **Weight**
    - The force due to gravity acting downward through the aircraft’s center of gravity (CG).

**Moments (Torques)**  
Moments are generated when forces act at a distance from the CG:
- **Rolling Moment**: Caused by asymmetric lift.
- **Pitching Moment**: Caused by imbalances in lift or thrust.
- **Yawing Moment**: Caused by lateral forces, such as those from the rudder.
