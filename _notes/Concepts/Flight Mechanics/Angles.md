## Angle of Attack (AoA)
- The angle between the chord line of an airfoil and the relative airflow.
- Critical for determining lift generation; beyond the critical AoA, a stall occurs.
- Directly affects lift and drag forces.

## Side-Slip Angle (β)
- The angle between the aircraft's longitudinal axis and the relative wind in the horizontal plane.
- Occurs when the aircraft's nose is not aligned with the flight path, such as in crosswind landings or uncoordinated turns.
- Side-slip generates lateral aerodynamic forces, impacting stability and control.
