# Torsion

## Definition

- Using Prandtl stress function $\phi$ 
$$
\tau_{xz} = \frac{\partial\phi}{\partial y};\,\,\, \tau_{yz} = - \frac{\partial\phi}{\partial x}
$$
- Compatibility conditions: 
$$
\frac{\partial^2\phi}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2\phi}{\partial y^2} = -2G\alpha
$$
- Maximum shear stress occurs at $y=\pm \frac{t}{2}$,
$$
(\tau_{xz})_{max} = G\alpha t
$$
- The torque is given as
$$
T = GJ\alpha
$$
- The equivalent J for a section is given as
$$
J = J_{1} + J_{2}
$$
- The J of a rectangular section is given as
$$
J = \frac{bt^3}{3}
$$
- Where
	- $b$ is the width of the section
	- $t$ is the thickness of the section.
