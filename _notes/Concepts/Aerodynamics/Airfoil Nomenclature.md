# Airfoil Nomenclature 
*Created on:* 2025-01-13 
*Tags:* #Nomeclature
*Course:* [[Aerodynamics]]

---
## Definition

- *Mean Camber Line:* It is the locus of points halfway between the upper and lower surfaces.
- *Leading Edge:* The most forward point on the mean camber line.
- *Trailing Edge:* The most rearward point on the mean camber line.
- *Chord Line:* The straight line connecting the leading and trailing edge.
- *Camber:* The maximum distance between the mean camber line and chord line.
- *Thickness:* It is the distance between the upper and lower surface.

## 4-Digit Series

- First Digit - Maximum camber percentage of the chord.
- Second Digit - Location of maximum chamber in tenths of the chord.
- Last two digits - Maximum thickness in percentage of the chord.

Example: NACA 2412 has maximum camber of 2%c at 0.4c and the maximum thickness is 12%.

## 5-digit Series

- First Digit - Product with 1.5 gives the design lift coefficient in tenths.
- 2nd and 3rd Digit - Product with 0.5 gives the maximum camber location in percentage of chord.
- 4th and 5th Digit - Maximum thickness in percentage of the chord.

Example: NACA 23012 has a design lift coefficient of 0.3. The maximum camber is located at 15%c and the maximum thickness is 12%c.
