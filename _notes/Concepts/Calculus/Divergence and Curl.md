# Divergence and Curl 
*Created on:* 2024-12-24  
*Tags:* #Divergence #Curl
*Course:* [[Calculus]]

---
## Definition

The divergence (flux density) of a vector field $F = M \hat{i} + N \hat{j} + O \hat{k}$ at the point is 
$$
div\,\,\mathbf{F} = \frac{\partial M}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial N}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial O}{\partial z}  
$$

The curl (circulation density) of a vector field $F = M \hat{i} + N \hat{j} + O \hat{k}$ at the point is 
$$
\nabla \times F = \begin{vmatrix}
\hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k}\\
\frac{\partial}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial}{\partial z}\\
M & N & O
\end{vmatrix}
$$
