# Mean Value Theorem 
*Created on:* 2024-12-23  
*Tags:* #MeanValueTheorem
*Course:* [[Calculus]]

---
## Definition

Suppose $y=f(x)$ is continuous on a closed interval $[a,b]$ and differentiable on the interval's interior $(a,b)$. Then there is at least one point $c$ in $(a,b)$ at which 
$$
\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a} = f'(c)
$$

A special version of the theorem is *Rolle's Theorem*, which states that if $f(a)=f(b)$, then $f'(c) =0$.
