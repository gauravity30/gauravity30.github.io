## Definition

The gradient vector of $f(x,y)$ at a point $P_{0}(x_{0},y_{0})$ is the vector
$$
\nabla f = \frac{df}{dx} \hat{i} + \frac{df}{dy} \hat{j}
$$
Obtained by evaluating the partial derivatives of $f$ at $P_{0}$.

The gradient vector provides the direction of most rapid increase of a function i.e. *a function $f$ increases most rapidly in the direction of gradient vector*.

At every point $(x_{0},y_{0})$ in the domain of a differentiable function $f(x,y)$, the gradient of $f$ is normal to the level curve through $(x_{0},y_{0})$.
