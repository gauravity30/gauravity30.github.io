# Thermophiles 
*Created on:* 2024-12-06  
*Tags:* #thermophiles
*Course:* [[Astrobiology]]

---
## Definition

*Organisms that thrive in extreme temperatures*
- *Mesophiles:* Growth in $15^{\circ}C-45^{\circ}C$
- *Thermophile:* Growth in $45^{\circ}C-80^{\circ}C$
- *Hyper-thermophiles:* Growth in $>80^{\circ}C$

### Hyperthermophiles

- First discovered in 1965 in the hot spring of Yellow Stone national park, Wyoming (US).
- >90 species of hyper thermophilic archaea and bacteria known.
- Examples: *Geothermobacterium ferrireducens* (Bacteria) and *Pyrolobus fumarii* (Archaea)

### Damaging effects of heat

- Damage to cytoskeleton
- Golgi bodies and Endoplasmic Reticulum become fragmented and disassemble.
- Mitochondria and lysosomes reduces and becomes weak.
- The nucleoli swells and large granular depositions of ribosomal proteins appear.
- Cell growth stops and leads to cell-cycle arrest.
- Membrane morphology changes and membrane fluidity increases.
  
### How thermophiles adapt ?

-  Form more *ion-bonds* which hold the proteins together.
- Create a *hydrophobic core* which prevents unfolding/denaturation at higher temperature.
- More *hydrogen bonds* keeps the structure strong.
- Create more *disulfide bonds* which stabilizes the protein structures.
