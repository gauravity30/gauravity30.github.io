# Moon
*Created on:* 2024-12-12 
*Tags:* #Moon
*Course:* [[Astrobiology]]

---
### Properties of Moon

- 1/4 Diameter of Earth
- 1/6 of Earth's gravity
- No active wind/water erosion
- Impacts
- Buckets of craters
- No active volcanos
- Small moonquakes.
- No active magnetic field
- No plate tectonics
- No liquid water
- Geologically inactive.

### Features of Moon

- Highlands: 
	- Older surface
	- More craters
	- Rough
	- More craters
- Mare: 
	- Newer surface
	- Fewer craters
	- Flat
	- Dark Basalt
- Riles/Lava tubes:
	- Long, deep leftover lava channels.
	- Hollow from inside.
	- Potential site for astrobiology.

**Permanently Shadowed Regions**: The floors of craters which never receive sunlight. Have water ice-deposits. Good for astrobiological experiments.
