## Definition

- Primordial Soup in the pools of water
- Hydrothermal vents deep in the ocean
- Ice-sheets
- Outer Space

### Phases of Earth

- *Hadean* 
	- 4-5 Billion Years Ago (BYA)
	- Hellish conditions in the young earth
	- Earth was still forming
- *Archaean*
	- 4-2.5 BYA
	- Single cell organisms
	- First fossil microbes
	- Ancient Life
- *Proterozoic*
	- 2.5-0.54 BYA
	- Oldest eukarya
	- Still single-celled creatures
	- Rise of oxygen
	- Plants that consumed $CO_{2}$ and produced $O_{2}$
	- Earlier life
- *Phanerozoic*
	- 0.54 - 0 BYA
	- Beginning marked by the cambrian period
	- Multi-cell organisms
	- Fossils visible to the naked eye
	- Many subdivisions based on fossil record
	- Visible Life
	  
[[Extremophiles]] help us predict the boundaries of life.
