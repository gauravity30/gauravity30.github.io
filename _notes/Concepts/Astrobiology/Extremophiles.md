# Extremophiles
*Created on:* 2024-12-06  
*Tags:* #extremophiles
*Course:* [[Astrobiology]]

---
## Definition
*An organism with the ability to thrive in extreme environments.*

**Extreme Locations**
- Polar Regions and Glaciers
- Hot-springs
- Hydrothermal vents
- Hyperacidic lakes
- Volcanoes
- Deserts
- Acid Mine Drainage
- Nuclear contaminated sites
- Soda Lakes and Hypersaline lakes
- Trenches
  
**Extreme environments**
- Temperatures - High or Low --> *[[Thermophiles]]* or *Psychrophile*
- Pressure - High --> *[[Barophiles]]*
- Salinity - High --> *Osmophiles*
- pH - High or Low --> *Alkaliphiles* or *Acidophiles*
- Water availability - low --> *Xerophiles*
- Lack of oxygen --> *Anaerobes* and *Microaerophiles*
- Nutrient availability - low --> *Oligotroph*
- Ionizing radiations - high --> *Toxitolerants*
- Toxic compounds - high --> *Toxitolerants*
- Heavy metals - high --> *Toxitolerants*

*Endoliths:* An organism that lives inside rock or in the pores between mineral grains.
