# Barophiles 
*Created on:* 2024-12-06  
*Tags:* #barophiles
*Course:* [[Astrobiology]]

---
## Definition

*Organisms that thrive under high pressure conditions and exhibit optimal reproduction rate at pressures greater than 0.1 MPa*.

- First bacterium isolated - *spirillum* (from Japan)
- *Halomonas salaria* requires 1000 times atm of pressure to stay alive.
- *Tardigrade* is the extreme extremophile. It can survive in all kinds of extreme environments.
  
**Types**
- *Barotolerants:* grows at pressure of 100-400 atm.
- *Barophilic:* grows at pressure greater than 400 atm.
- *Extreme barophilic:* grows at pressure higher than 700 atm.
### Life under extreme pressures

- It is very dark and cold.
- *Twilight Zone:* Light levels are low for photosynthesis below 200 m.
- *Midnight Zone:* Sunlight doesn't reach below 1000 m.
- Low levels of oxygen and density of organisms is very low.
- 25% of all organisms live in the ocean depths and 91% of them are undiscovered.
- Very high pressure approximately 400 atms.
  
### Adaptations in Barophiles

- Lipids contain unsaturated fatty acids.
- Protein coat on the exterior layer.
- Proteins/enzymes which hold the cellular structure together.
- Protein synthesis through *Gene expression* is pressure dependent.

### Adaptations in piezophiles

- Protein adaptation which maintains a balance between the imperative stability (higher # of bonds) to be functional and the flexibility (less # of bonds).
- Cavities of small size allows water penetration at high pressures.
- Presence of osmolytes (sugar and amino acids) to protect cell.
- High-pressure nervous syndrome.
