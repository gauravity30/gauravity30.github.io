## Definition
*Biosignatures* are used to determine if something is alive in the first place. Biosignatures can be.
- Can be fossilized cells. @biosignatures
- Active microbial communities. @biosignatures
- Molecules that are made only by living organisms
  
*Biosignatures* are molecules that would be out of place in a planet's geochemistry if it were not for some living organism churning them out @biosignatures.

### What is Life ?

*A self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution* 
It is a system of molecules that can reproduce, store information, and generate energy through metabolizing molecules in its environment. @biosignatures

Determination of a molecule from space or ancient earth being *biosignature* is based on this definition of *life*, known as *Ladder of Life Detection* @biosignatures.

*Life is choosy*, it prefers some molecules over others, so its presence can change the distribution of chemical species on a planet.
Example, Life of on earth prefers lighter isotopes of carbon in biomolecules. Thus, carbon-13 and carbon-14 in organisms differs from their relative abundances in planet in general @biosignatures.

### Ladder of Life

Features suggesting presence of life ascend from weak (bottom) to strong (top) on the ladder of life,

1. *Darwinian Evolution*: Culture of microbes that show signs of adaptation to selective pressure.
2. *Growth and Reproduction:* Observation of a suspected microbes at multiple stages of its life cycle.
3. *Metabolism:* Extraction of energy from molecules in the environment.
4. *Functional molecules and structures:* Class of molecules that might support information that might support information storage or other biological function.
5. *Potential biomolecule components:* Chemical compounds that could make up complex biomolecules.
6. *Potential metabolic by-products:* These complex accumulate in a distinct way in the environment or contain features that follow a pattern suggesting a living metabolic cycle at play.
7. *Biofabrics:* Structures like mats or layered morphologies created by microbial colonies.
