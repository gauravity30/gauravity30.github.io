## Definition
*Composite index comprising of the transfer function of the propellant to generate high pressure gases and the expansion of the gases in the nozzle.*

$$
I_{sp} = \frac{F_{t}}{\dot{m}} = C^* \times C_{f}
$$
It is the product of [[Characteristic Velocity]] and [[Thrust Coefficient]].

OR

It is  [[Rocket Thrust]] per [[Mass Flow Rate]].
