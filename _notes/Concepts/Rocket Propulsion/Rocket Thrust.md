# Thrust Equation  
*Created on:* 2024-11-28  
*Tags:* #Thrust 
*Course:* [[Rocket Propulsion]]

---
## Definition
*The thrust equation for rockets is given as*
$$
T = \dot{m}V_{e} + (p_{e} - p_{a})A_{e}
$$

$$
\implies T = \dot{m}\left(V_{e} + \frac{(p_{e} - p_{a})A_{e}}{\dot{m}}\right)
$$
$$
\implies T = \dot{m}V_{j}
$$
Where,
- $T$ is the thrust.
- $\dot{m}$ is the [[Mass Flow Rate]].
- $V_{e}$ is the nozzle exit velocity.
- $V_{j}$ is the effective [[Exhaust Velocity]].
