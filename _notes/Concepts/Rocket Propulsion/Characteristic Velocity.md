# Characteristic Velocity

## Definition
*Index of performance of the propellant. Represented as $C^*$*

From the definition of [[Mass Flow Rate]],

$$
\dot{m} = \frac{\sqrt{\gamma}}{\sqrt{R T_{c}}} \left(\frac{2}{\gamma + 1}\right)^\frac{\gamma+1}{2(\gamma-1)} P_{c} A_{t}
$$

$$
\frac{1}{C^*} = \frac{\dot{m}}{P_{c} A_{t}} = \frac{\sqrt{\gamma}}{\sqrt{R T_{c}}} \left(\frac{2}{\gamma + 1}\right)^\frac{\gamma+1}{2(\gamma-1)} 
$$

The pressure ( $P_c$ ) and mass flow rate ($\dot{m}$) can be measured and hence provide an actual $C^*$.

Whereas, the definition based on chamber temperature ($T_{c}$) provide the theoretical value of $C^*$.
