# Cooling of thrust chamber  
*Created on:* 2024-11-28  
*Tags:* #coolingMechanism 
*Course:* [[Rocket Propulsion]]

---
## Definition
- *Film and Barrier cooling*
	- A thin film of coolant  is made to flow along the inner wall of the thrust chamber.
	- Coolant absorbs heat from the wall.
	- In film cooling, coolant can be either oxidizer or the fuel.
	- In barrier cooling, coolant is the fuel rich product of combustion.
- *Regenerative Cooling*
	- Liquid fuel or oxidizer is used to cool the thrust chamber walls externally.
	- Enhances the temperature and enthalpy of the fuel during cooling.
- *Ablation Cooling*
	- Insulating materials which erode at high temperatures, have been used to protect walls of the thrust chamber from high temperature products of combustion.
- *Transpiration Cooling*
	- Liquid propellants are made to ooze through fine pores in the material.
