# Nitramine Propellants  
*Created on:* 2024-11-28

---
## Definition
*Formed by adding highly explosive material like HMX and RDX to the fuel binder instead of AP*

- Low thermal signature and used for strategic applications.
- Polymeric binder acts as a coolant instead of burning the fuel-rich products of HMX/RDX.
