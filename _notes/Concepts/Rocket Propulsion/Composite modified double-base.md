# Composite Modified Double Base (CMDB)  
*Created on:* 2024-11-28
*Tags:* #CMDB
*Course:* [[Rocket Propulsion]]

---
## Definition
*Propellants formed by adding explosives like AP or HMX to [[Double-base]] propellants.*

- Used in missiles, upper stage solid propellant rockets.
- *AP* reduces the fuel richness and improves the [[Specific Impulse]]. 
- *HMX* improves the energy release.
- More rugged than [[Double-base]].
