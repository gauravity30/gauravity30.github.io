# Flow Separation in Rocket Nozzle 
*Created on:* 2024-11-28  
*Tags:* #FlowSeparation #Nozzle
*Course:* [[Rocket Propulsion]]

---
## Definition
*Flow separation may occur based on the nozzle exit pressure $p_{n}$.*

[Summerfield Criterion for onset of flow separation] provides an empirical relation to predict the flow separation.

$$
\frac{p_{n}}{p_{a}} = (1.88M_{c} - 1)^{-0.64}
$$

Responsible for performance losses, another kind of performance loss is [[Divergence Loss]].
