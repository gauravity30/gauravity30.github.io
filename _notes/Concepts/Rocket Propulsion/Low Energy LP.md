# Low Energy LP  
*Created on:* 2024-11-28
*Tags:* #lowEnergyLP
*Course:* [[Rocket Propulsion]]

---
## Definition
*Propellants with [[Specific Impulse]] less than 3000 Ns/kg*

- *Nitric Acid - Oxidizer*
	- Red fuming Nitric Acid ($HNO_3$ with 15% $NO_{2}$)
	- White fuming Nitric Acid ($HNO_3$ with 0.5% $NO_{2}$)
	- Highly corrosive

- *Hydrazine - Fuel*
	- Positive heat of formation
	- Can be used as a monopropellant.
	- Reacts readily with $HNO_{3}$ and $N_{2}O_{4}$ giving high heat release, forms products with low molecular mass.
	- Carcinogenic
	- MMH - Mono-methyl hydrazine
	- UDMH - Unsymmetrical di-methyl hydrazine.
	- MMH and UMDH with $HNO_3$ and $N_2 O_4$
	  
- *Kerosene - Fuel*
	- Consists of several hydrocarbons
	- Combustion inefficient due to presence of aromatics.
	- Aviation fuel/Pure Kerosene
	- Jet mix product (JP)
	- Rocket Propellant (RP)
	- Kerosene -LO2 - Semi-cryogenic propellant.
