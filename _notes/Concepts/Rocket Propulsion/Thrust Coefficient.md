# Thrust Coefficient 
*Created on:* 2024-11-28 
*Tags:* #ThrustCoefficient 
*Course:* [[Rocket Propulsion]]

---
## Definition
*Measure of amplification of force at nozzle due to the nozzle. Represented as $C_{f}$*

**How good the nozzle is ?**

From the definition of [[Rocket Thrust]], [[Mass Flow Rate]] and [[Exhaust Velocity]],

$$
F_{T} = P_{c} A_{t} \sqrt{ \frac{2\gamma^2}{\gamma-1} \left(\frac{2\gamma}{\gamma -1}\right)^\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1} \left(1-\left(\frac{P_{e}}{P_{c}}\right)^\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma} \right)} + \left(\frac{P_{e}}{P_{c}} - \frac{P_{a}}{P_{c}} \right) \frac{A_{e}}{A_{t}}
$$
$$
F_{T} = P_{c} A_{t} C_{f}
$$
