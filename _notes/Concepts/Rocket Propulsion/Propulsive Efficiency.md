# Propulsive Efficiency

## Definition
*Propulsive Efficiency determines how of KE of the exhaust jet is useful for propelling a vehicle*

OR

*Ratio of rate of work done by the rocket to the rate of the energy supplied.*

Rate of work done is the product of thrust and [[Exhaust Velocity]].

$$
\eta_{p} = \frac{\dot{m} V_{j} V}{\dot{m} V_{j} V + 0.5 \dot{m}(V-V_{j})^2} = \frac{2\left( \frac{V}{V_{j}} \right)}{1+\left( \frac{V}{V_{j}} \right)^2}
$$
