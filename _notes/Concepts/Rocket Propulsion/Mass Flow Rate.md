## Definition
*The mass flow rate is defined as the mass of fluid flowing through the throat in a unit time.*

$$
\dot{m} = \rho_{t} A_{t} V_{t}
$$

Using [[Compressible Flow Relations]], 

$$
\dot{m} = \frac{\sqrt{\gamma}}{\sqrt{R T_{c}}} \left(\frac{2}{\gamma + 1}\right)^\frac{\gamma+1}{2(\gamma-1)} P_{c} A_{t}
$$
