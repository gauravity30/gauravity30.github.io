## Definition
*Following are the criteria for selection of rocket propulsion device,*
- Mission Requirements
- Affordability (Cost)
- Reliability
- Maintainability
- Operability
- Controllability
- Prior Experience
- Geometric Constraints
- Schedule
- Environmental Impact
- Reusability
- Survivability
- System Performance
