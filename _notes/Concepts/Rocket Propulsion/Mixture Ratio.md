# Mixture Ratio  
*Created on:* 2024-11-28
*Tags:* #MixtureRatio
*Course:* [[Rocket Propulsion]]

---
## Definition
*The ratio of mass of the oxidizer to the mass of the fuel taking part in combustion*

$$
MR = \frac{M_{ox}}{M_{f}}
$$

Mixture ratio corresponding to stoichiometric composition is referred as $MR_{stoi}$.

- *Fuel Rich:* $MR  < MR_{stoi}$ 
- *Oxidizer Rich:* $MR  > MR_{stoi}$  

**Products of Combustion:**
- *Stoichiometric or Oxidizer Rich*: Complete combustion. Carbon is converted into $CO_{2}$.
- *Fuel Rich:* Incomplete combustion. Carbon is converted to $CO$ and $CO_{2}$.
