# Mass Fraction
*Created on:* 2024-11-27
*Tags:* #massRatio #massFraction #RocketPropulsion
*Course:* [[Rocket Propulsion]]

---
## Definition
- *Mass Fraction* $R_{m} = \frac{M_{f}}{M_{i}}$
- *Payload Mass Fraction* $\alpha$
- *Structural Mass Fraction* $\beta$
- *Propellant Mass Fraction* $\gamma$

$$
\alpha + \beta + \gamma = 1
$$

$$
R_{m} = \alpha + \beta
$$ From [[Rocket Equation]],

$$
\boxed{\Delta V = V_{j}\ln\left(\frac{1}{\alpha + \beta}\right)}
$$

$$
\alpha = e^{\Delta V / V_{j}} - \beta
$$ To achieve higher value of payload mass fraction,
- $V_{j}$ needs to be larger i.e. more [[Exhaust Velocity]]
- $\beta$ needs to be small i.e. less structure mass
