# Efficiency of Rocket Engine  
*Created on:* 2024-11-28

---
## Definition
*Efficiency of the rocket engine is defined as the ratio of the actual thrust and ideal thrust.*

- *$C^*$ Efficiency:* The ratio of actual [[Characteristic Velocity]] to the theoretical value.
  
  $$
\eta_{C^*} = \frac{C^*_{actual}}{C^*_{ideal}}
   $$
  
- *Thrust Correction Coefficient:* The ratio of actual [[Thrust Coefficient]] to the ideal value.

$$
\zeta_{f} = \frac{C_{F,actual}}{C_{F,ideal}}
$$


Now, 

$$
\frac{F_{a}}{F_{i}} = \eta_{C^*} \times \zeta_{F}
$$
