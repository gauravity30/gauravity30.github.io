## Definition

- The gas turbine is optimized to produce shaft horsepower rather than jet thrust.
- The turboshaft engine are very similar to turbojets with additional turbine expansion to extract heat energy from the exhaust and convert it into output shaft power.
- They are similar to turboprops.
- Used in high power output, high reliability, small size and light weight operations.
- Helicopters, APU, boats, ships, etc.
