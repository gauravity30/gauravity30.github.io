*Created on:* 2025-02-08

---
## Definition

- The attractiveness of a turbopropeller engine as compared with a turbofan engine lies in its ability to offer a very large bypass ratio.
- There is a limitation of flight Mach number for the engines due to the limitation on the tip Mach number of a rotating propeller.
- This limitation is not present in a turbofan engine.
- Operating Mach number for the engine is 0.4 to 0.8.
- It also adds to the engine weight and system complexity.
- The power from the turbine is used to run the propellor.
