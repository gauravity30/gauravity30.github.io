# Saint-Venant's Principle 
*Created on:* 2025-01-30  
*Tags:* #Saint-VenantsPrinciple
*Course:* [[MOS]]

---
## Definition

It states that the stress, strain, and displacement fields caused by two different statically equivalent force distributions on parts of the body far away from the loading points are approximately the same.
