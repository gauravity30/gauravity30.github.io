# Poisson's Ratio 
*Created on:* 2025-01-30  
*Tags:* #PoissonRatio

---
## Definition

- Ratio between the lateral deformation and traverse deformation.
- Compressing a block axially causes bulging of the block sideways.
- Definite property of a material. 

$$
\nu = -\frac{\text{lateral strain}}{\text{axial strain}}
$$
