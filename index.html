Gaurav Gupta (गौरव गुप्ता)

Scientist/Engineer at ISRO

Fluid dynamicist specializing in computational studies of biological and bio-inspired flow problems. Primary experience encompasses investigating avian flight aerodynamics, modeling microgravity two-phase flows, and developing custom flow-solver and post-processing subroutines in Fortran and Python respectively. Currently working on capillary-driven flows and interfacial phenomena under microgravity at ISRO.

Research Interests: Biological fluid mechanics; animal locomotion; low-Reynolds number aerodynamics; vortex dynamics; fluid-structure interactions; turbulent and interfacial flows.
